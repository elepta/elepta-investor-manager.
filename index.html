Companies

Record company profit or loss

Each profit entry stores the company allocation percentage effective when it is recorded. Editing a company rate applies to new entries only. Use a negative number for a loss. Profit distributions cannot be created for a period with zero or negative combined profit.

Calculate and save profit distribution

Each investor is subject to the category-specific profit cap effective for the selected month. Any excess remains unallocated. Distribution is calculated using investor capital balances at the end of the selected month. This is a month-end snapshot, not daily time-weighted allocation. Review and approve before recording any payment. Saving a distribution records a payable amount, not a bank transfer.
